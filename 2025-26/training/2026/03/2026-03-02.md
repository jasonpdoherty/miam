# Training Session - Monday 9th February 2026

**Duration:** 60 mins

**Focus:** Endurance + finding space off the ball

---

## WARM UP LAPS (5 mins)
2-3 easy laps. Dynamic stretches on the move - high knees, heel flicks, side shuffles.

---

## RONDO

![alt text](rondo.png)

**Setup:** Bibs of 3, keeping the ball between 2, other trying to intercept, 2 sets of 3 together

**Scoring:** After 5 passes score in either end for a point

**Coaching notes:** Promotes movement off the ball, awareness of others

---

## QUADRANT BALL - NETBALL/AMERICAN FOOTBALL HYBRID (25 mins)


![alt text](quadrant.png)

**Setup:** Use full width, ~30-40m long. Cones for end zones at each end.

**Progression:**
- Start with unlimited touches before passing
- Then limit to 3 seconds max hold
- Then add "must complete 5 passes before you can score"

**Coaching notes:** Focus on movement off the ball - if you're standing still, you're useless. Encourage quick passing, checking shoulders, and creating angles. Call out good runs into space. Penalise lazy movement by giving the ball to the other team.

---

## MATCH (15 mins if time)
Normal rules. Let them apply the movement patterns from the space ball game.
