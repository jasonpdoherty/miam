# Training Session - Monday 9th February 2026

**Duration:** 60 mins

**Focus:** Endurance + finding space off the ball

---

## WARM UP LAPS (5 mins)
2-3 easy laps. Dynamic stretches on the move - high knees, heel flicks, side shuffles.

---

## RONDO RACE (20 mins)
Split into two teams. One team does a rondo (with a neutral or 1-2 defenders from the other team), while the rest of their team runs laps. You can only run when your team has the ball - if possession is lost, you stop.

**Setup:** Rondo in the middle, running track around the pitch. Swap teams after a set time (e.g. 4-5 mins).

**Scoring:** Count laps completed by each team. Team with most laps after both rotations wins.

**Coaching notes:** Creates real pressure on the rondo - lose the ball and your runners are stuck. Encourages sharp, safe passing but also quick recovery when possession is lost. Runners need to pay attention to the rondo, not just zone out.

---

## SPACE BALL - NETBALL/AMERICAN FOOTBALL HYBRID (25 mins)
Two teams. Score by catching the ball in the end zone. No running with the ball - must pass immediately when you receive it. Defenders can intercept but no contact.

**Setup:** Use full width, ~30-40m long. Cones for end zones at each end.

**Progression:**
- Start with unlimited touches before passing
- Then limit to 3 seconds max hold
- Then add "must complete 5 passes before you can score"

**Coaching notes:** Focus on movement off the ball - if you're standing still, you're useless. Encourage quick passing, checking shoulders, and creating angles. Call out good runs into space. Penalise lazy movement by giving the ball to the other team.

---

## MATCH (15 mins if time)
Normal rules. Let them apply the movement patterns from the space ball game.
